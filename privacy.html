Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the responsible handling of information are important to Alipin AI.

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how Alipin AI may collect, use and protect information when you use our application and related services.

1. Introduction

Alipin AI ("Alipin AI", "we", "us", or "our") is an AI-assisted content automation tool designed to help users discover mobile accessories, prepare product information, generate content, and manage Pinterest publishing workflows.

By using Alipin AI, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Alipin AI, the application may process limited information necessary to provide its functionality.

Information provided by you

  • Name or basic account information, if provided.
  • Email address when you contact us.
  • Product URLs or product information you choose to process.
  • Content or instructions you provide to AI-assisted features.

Pinterest account information

If you connect a Pinterest account through an authorized integration, Alipin AI may receive information and permissions provided through Pinterest's authorization process.

We only use Pinterest-related information for the functionality that you authorize, such as preparing or publishing content to your Pinterest account.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Alipin AI may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the application's features.
  • Finding and organizing relevant product information.
  • Generating AI-assisted titles, descriptions and keywords.
  • Preparing content for Pinterest publishing.
  • Publishing content when you have authorized the application.
  • Responding to support and privacy-related requests.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the service.
  • Preventing misuse, fraud or unauthorized activity.

4. Pinterest Integration

Alipin AI may use Pinterest's APIs and authorized integrations to provide Pinterest-related functionality.

When you connect your Pinterest account, you authorize Alipin AI to access only the permissions requested during the authorization process.

Pinterest account information is used only for providing the functionality you have authorized. We do not sell Pinterest account information or use it for unrelated purposes.

Your use of Pinterest is also subject to Pinterest's own terms, policies and privacy practices.

5. Product Information and Affiliate Links

Alipin AI may process publicly available product information, including product names, descriptions, prices, images and product URLs, when such information is used for content creation.

Some product links generated or published through the service may contain affiliate tracking parameters. If you make a purchase through an affiliate link, the applicable merchant or affiliate program may receive information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Alipin AI does not process or store your payment card information for purchases made through third-party merchants.

6. AI-Assisted Content Generation

Alipin AI may use third-party artificial intelligence services to assist with generating titles, descriptions, keywords and other content.

Information submitted to AI-assisted features may be processed by the relevant service provider according to its applicable terms and privacy policies.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the requested content-generation task.

7. Third-Party Services

Alipin AI may interact with third-party platforms and services, including Pinterest, product/affiliate platforms and AI service providers.

These third parties operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, terms and data-handling practices. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party service they use through Alipin AI.

8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Alipin AI.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may also stop using Alipin AI or revoke the application's access to your Pinterest account through the relevant Pinterest account settings.

11. Data Deletion Requests

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your use of Alipin AI, contact us using the email address below.
